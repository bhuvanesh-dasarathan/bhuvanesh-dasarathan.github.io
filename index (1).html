Methods in, evidence out.

Four studies, one path: M&E methods collect the data, analysis interprets it, and triangulated findings lead to an outcome. Partner names are withheld.

Evaluation

Child labour baseline assessment

International labour-rights organisation

A baseline for floriculture supply chains across 15 villages in 5 districts: child protection, wages, grievance mechanisms and occupational safety.

LFA + ToCFindings built into an LFA and Theory of Change that now shape the programme.

M&E methods

KIIs and SSIs with farmers, workers, school staff and officials, FGDs with communities, and a 900-respondent survey.

KII · SSI · FGD · Survey

Analysis

Analysis by theme and village, thematic coding of interviews, benchmarked against ASER 2024.

Advanced Excel · SQL · Power BI

Spraying without protective kit55%
Paid below statutory wage30%

District government partnership · CSR-funded

A village-level assessment of bonded labour households in two districts: wages against statutory rates, working conditions, safety, health and child involvement.

04Built by me

Tools I designed and built.

Beyond the analysis: a data collection platform used across every programme, and the public website for our M&E team.

Data collection tool · Hand in Hand India

Survey Speaks.

An offline-capable data collection tool I designed and built by studying the strengths and gaps of mWater, KoboToolbox, Google Forms and Microsoft Forms. Collected data flows into linked databases and spreadsheets, is cleaned and analysed, and feeds Power BI and Tableau dashboards and donor reports.
